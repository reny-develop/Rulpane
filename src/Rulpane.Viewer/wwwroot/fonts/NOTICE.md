# Bundled fonts

The latin subsets of [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter) and
[JetBrains Mono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono), as variable fonts, taken from
Fontsource. Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: see
[`Inter-OFL.txt`](Inter-OFL.txt) and [`JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt`](JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt).

They are served from here because the page's Content-Security-Policy loads nothing from another
origin. Japanese text falls back to the system font.
